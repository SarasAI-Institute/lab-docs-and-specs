- What did I think the starter did, and what did I verify in the source or by running it?
- What context did I give my coding assistant, and why?
- Which prompt changed after new evidence? Include the relevant prompt/output or a local link.
- What did I accept, reject or correct, and why?
- What command or action checked the result? What actually happened?
- Which commits preserve the important steps?
- What method will I apply to my own PromptLab work?
